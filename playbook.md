## 6. Credit and permission rules

1. Public posts only. Skip anything protected, deleted or taken down.
2. Credit every clip on its own line: `🎥 @creator`. Official: `🎥 @RockstarGames`. Photos: `📸 @creator`.
3. Credit is not permission. Ask reachable creators first; prefer those who allow sharing.
4. Transform: trim, hook, captions, our commentary or tip. Never re-upload a whole video.
5. Takedown request = remove same day, log in `takedowns.md`, never use that creator again without permission.
6. Never post leaks, datamined files, leaked builds, pirated copies, "play early" tricks or exploits.
7. Don't name leak outlets or rumor sources.
8. Rockstar's footage is their property. Never imply we're Rockstar.

## 7. Clipping specs

Hook in the first second, 7 to 30 s, big burned-in captions in the safe area, our own value added, no spoilers in hook or thumbnail, label `SPOILER` in caption. Export MP4, H.264, AAC, 1080x1920 (9:16), 1080p max.

## 12. Rights and platform risk (added 2026-11-18)

- Take-Two says it does not object to fan use of its material for **non-commercial** purposes. Monetizing Rockstar footage is outside that, so expect possible takedowns; licensing requests go to copyright@take2games.com. Get advice before monetizing official footage.
- Never clip isolated story cutscenes, ending reveals, or edited story sequences: Rockstar removes these.
- YouTube age-restricts game clips focused on torture or mass violence against non-combatants. No "rampage" clips.
- Never reclip a repost account. Trace every clip to the original creator, ask permission, credit them.
- Accounts marked AVOID in `watchlist.md` are never a source.
