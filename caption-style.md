# Viral caption and hashtag style

Owner request (2026-11-18): every caption is a viral-style caption with viral GTA 6 hashtags, a description, and a tips / in-game money angle where it fits.

**"Money" always means in-game money** (GTA dollars, heists, businesses in Leonida). Never imply viewers can earn real money from the game or from us. Never post an in-game money method until we've seen it in real gameplay (after launch) and it's in `facts.md` or a credited gameplay source.

## Caption formula

```
[HOOK: 3 to 8 words, curiosity or payoff, 1 emoji max]
[1 to 2 lines of value: the tip, the detail, or why to watch]
[CTA: question or "save this" / "follow for more GTA 6 tips"]
[hashtags]
```

Hook patterns that work:
- "Nobody is talking about this in GTA 6 👀"
- "Do THIS first in GTA 6"
- "The fastest way to make money in GTA 6 💰" (only once verified)
- "You missed this in GTA 6"
- "GTA 6 tip that saves you hours"
- "Wait for it... 💀"
- "POV: your first night in Vice City 🌴"

CTAs: "Save this for launch night", "Follow @earnongta6 for daily GTA 6 tips", "What would you do first? 👇", "Tag the friend who needs this".

## Hashtags

Core (pick 2 to 3 every time): `#GTA6` `#GTAVI` `#GTA6Tips` `#GrandTheftAuto6`
Topic (pick 1 to 2 that match):
- Money: `#GTA6Money` `#GTA6MoneyGuide` `#GTA6MoneyMethod`
- Tips/secrets: `#GTA6Tips` `#GTA6Secrets` `#GTA6EasterEggs` `#GTA6Guide`
- Gameplay: `#GTA6Gameplay` `#GTA6Clips`
- Places/people: `#ViceCity` `#Leonida` `#Lucia` `#JasonDuval`
- Cars: `#GTA6Cars`
- Funny: `#GTA6Funny` `#GTA6Moments`
Reach (TikTok/Shorts only, 1 to 2): `#Gaming` `#GamingTips` `#fyp` `#viral`

Counts per platform:
- **TikTok:** 4 to 6 hashtags.
- **YouTube Shorts:** 3 to 5, put `#Shorts` first.
- **Instagram:** 3 to 5 (Instagram limits posts to 5).
- **X:** 2 hashtags max (`#GTA6` plus one topic tag). The owner asked for hashtags everywhere. More than 2 tends to cut reach on X.

Never: hashtags for things not in the clip, "leak" or "early access" tags, other brands' or creators' tags as bait, or the same caption twice in a week.
