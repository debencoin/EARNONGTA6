## Platform rules to know first
- **Take-Two:** "does not object" to fan use of its material for **non-commercial** purposes. Monetized use is outside that; licensing: copyright@take2games.com. Rockstar removes isolated cutscene videos, story sequences, ending reveals and leaked footage. (Find the official policy page and add it to facts.md.)
- **YouTube (from Nov 17):** age-restricts game clips focused on torture or mass violence against non-combatants. No "rampage" clips. https://www.pcgamer.com/software/platforms/youtube-updates-its-policy-on-violent-videogame-clips-with-guidelines-that-seem-aimed-at-the-incoming-flood-of-gta-6-videos
